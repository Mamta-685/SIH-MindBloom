# MindBloom Demo Script

## 2-Minute Demo Flow for Hackathon Presentation

### Pre-Demo Setup (30 seconds)
- Open browser to `http://localhost:5173`
- Ensure app is running smoothly
- Have backup screenshots ready
- Test audio if available

---

### 1. Landing Screen (15 seconds)
**What to say:**
> "Welcome to MindBloom, a mental wellness companion designed specifically for students. Notice how it greets you warmly and asks about your feelings without any forced signup."

**What to do:**
- Enter a name (e.g., "Alex")
- Select a mood (e.g., "Worried" 😟)
- Show the empathetic response
- Point out the smooth animations

**Key points to highlight:**
- No forced registration
- Empathetic, non-judgmental tone
- Beautiful, calming design

---

### 2. Dashboard (20 seconds)
**What to say:**
> "Here's your personalized dashboard. It remembers your mood and shows your growth journey through this beautiful tree visualization."

**What to do:**
- Show the mood display
- Point to the growth tree with leaves
- Highlight the quick action buttons
- Show recent mood history

**Key points to highlight:**
- Personalization
- Visual progress tracking
- Easy navigation

---

### 3. Breathing Exercise (30 seconds)
**What to say:**
> "Let's try the breathing exercise - a core feature for stress relief. Watch how the circle guides your breathing rhythm."

**What to do:**
- Click "Start Breathing"
- Show the animated circle expanding/contracting
- Point to the timer and progress bar
- Explain the 4-phase breathing cycle
- Let it run for 10-15 seconds

**Key points to highlight:**
- Guided mindfulness
- Visual breathing cues
- Stress reduction benefits
- Gamification (earns leaves)

---

### 4. Journal (25 seconds)
**What to say:**
> "The journal feature includes AI-powered sentiment analysis. It understands your emotions and responds with empathy."

**What to do:**
- Click "New Entry"
- Write a short entry (e.g., "Feeling stressed about exams")
- Show the sentiment analysis (Negative)
- Point out the empathetic response
- Show the entry in the list

**Key points to highlight:**
- AI sentiment analysis
- Empathetic responses
- Privacy (local storage)
- Emotional intelligence

---

### 5. Connect Board (20 seconds)
**What to say:**
> "Students can share support anonymously, building a positive community without revealing personal information."

**What to do:**
- Show existing posts
- Click "Share Support"
- Write a supportive message
- Post it
- Show it appears in the list

**Key points to highlight:**
- Anonymous community
- Supportive environment
- Mental health awareness
- Peer support

---

### 6. Growth Tree (10 seconds)
**What to say:**
> "Every positive action grows your tree. It's a visual representation of your mental wellness journey."

**What to do:**
- Show the tree with leaves
- Point to achievements
- Show progress bar
- Explain how activities add leaves

**Key points to highlight:**
- Gamification
- Progress visualization
- Positive reinforcement
- Long-term engagement

---

### Closing (10 seconds)
**What to say:**
> "MindBloom works offline, syncs across devices with Firebase, and includes accessibility features. It's designed to support student mental health with empathy and understanding."

**Key points to highlight:**
- Offline functionality
- Cross-device sync
- Accessibility
- Student-focused design

---

## Demo Tips

### Do:
- Speak clearly and confidently
- Show genuine enthusiasm
- Highlight the empathetic design
- Mention accessibility features
- Emphasize student focus

### Don't:
- Rush through features
- Get stuck on technical details
- Ignore the emotional impact
- Forget to mention offline capability
- Skip the growth tree visualization

### Time Management:
- Keep each section under 30 seconds
- Have a timer visible
- Practice the flow beforehand
- Prepare for questions

---

## Q&A Preparation

### Common Questions:

**Q: "How does it protect privacy?"**
A: "All data is stored locally by default. Firebase sync is optional and encrypted. No personal information is required."

**Q: "What makes it different from other apps?"**
A: "It's designed specifically for students, includes sentiment analysis, and focuses on empathy rather than just tracking."

**Q: "How does the sentiment analysis work?"**
A: "It uses a lightweight client-side library to analyze text and provide empathetic responses based on emotional tone."

**Q: "Can it replace therapy?"**
A: "No, it's a supportive tool. We encourage students to seek professional help when needed."

**Q: "Is it accessible?"**
A: "Yes, it includes dyslexia-friendly fonts, high contrast mode, and keyboard navigation."
